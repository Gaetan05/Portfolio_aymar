Derek Sivers started the idea of a **/now page**: instead of an "about" page frozen in time, a page that tells people what you’re focused on *at this point in your life*.

I loved it enough to add one to this site — with a twist: old snapshots are archived, so it doubles as a diary.

Use the `discovery` kind for anything you stumble upon and want to remember: a tool, a concept, a place, a person worth following.
