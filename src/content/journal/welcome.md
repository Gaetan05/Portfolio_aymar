Most portfolios are frozen in time: a list of jobs, a photo, a contact form. They say *what* someone did, rarely *who* they are.

I wanted something that grows with me.

## A garden, not a brochure

This site is organised like a garden. Some things are polished essays; others are seedlings — a quick note, a link I found, a thing I learned this morning. All of it is welcome here.

## What you’ll find

- **Projects** — work and side projects, including the ones that failed.
- **Journal** — essays, notes, discoveries and TILs.
- **Hobbies** — what I do for the love of it.
- **The Shelf** — books, films, tools and places I recommend.
- **Now** — a snapshot of what I’m focused on right now.

## The rule

Write it down. If it was interesting enough to think about, it’s interesting enough to note. Future me will thank present me.
